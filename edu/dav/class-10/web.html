The given pie chart represents the amount of money spent by a family on different items in a month. Write an analytical paragraph in 100–120 words using the information given in the pie chart.

Precision note: The chart shows expenditure, not household income.

Answer in Discussion Points

1

Introduce the chart

It presents a family’s monthly expenditure across six categories.

2

Highest share

Groceries account for the largest proportion at 23%.

3

Next category

Education ranks second at 20%, three points below groceries.

4

Middle values

Clothes and transportation are nearly equal at 19% and 18%.

5

Lower shares

Miscellaneous costs are 15%, while rent is lowest at 5%.

6

Overall inference

Essential daily and developmental needs dominate the budget.

Useful comparisons

  • Groceries exceed rent by 18 points.
  • Education and clothes differ by 1 point.
  • Clothes and transport differ by 1 point.
  • The four largest categories total 80%.
  • Groceries and education total 43%.
  • Rent is under one-fourth of groceries.
