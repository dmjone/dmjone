Class task

Write an analytical paragraph

The given pie chart represents the amount of money spent by a family on different items in a month. Write an analytical paragraph in 100–120 words using the information given in the pie chart.

Answer revealed
1

Introduce the chart

It shows a family’s monthly expenditure across six categories.

2

Highest share

Groceries receive the largest allocation at 23%.

3

Second category

Education is next at 20%, three points below groceries.

4

Middle values

Clothes and transportation are nearly equal at 19% and 18%.

5

Lower shares

Miscellaneous costs are 15%; rent is lowest at 5%.

6

Overall inference

Essential daily and developmental needs dominate the budget.

High-Value Comparisons

Precision: the chart shows expenditure, not income.

Useful figures

  • Groceries exceed rent by 18 points.
  • Education and clothes differ by 1 point.
  • Clothes and transport differ by 1 point.
  • Groceries + education = 43%.
  • Top four categories = 80%.
  • Rent is under one-fourth of groceries.
