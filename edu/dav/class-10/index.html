Read, compare, group and infer before writing the 100–120 word response.

Class task

Write an analytical paragraph

The given pie chart represents the amount of money spent by a family on different items in a month. Write an analytical paragraph in 100–120 words using the information given in the pie chart.

Use comparisons, not a list Stay factual and objective
Discuss before revealing
  • Largest and smallest shares
  • Closest percentage values
  • One useful combined total

Answer in Class Discussion Points

Build the paragraph from an overview, comparisons and a final inference.

Answer revealed
1

Introduce the chart

It presents the percentage distribution of a family’s monthly expenditure across six categories.

2

Identify the highest share

Groceries form the largest allocation at 23% of the total monthly spending.

3

Explain the next category

Education ranks second at 20%, only 3 percentage points below groceries.

4

Compare middle values

Clothes and transportation receive 19% and 18%, so their allocations are nearly equal.

5

Cover the lower shares

Miscellaneous expenses account for 15%, whereas rent is the smallest component at 5%.

6

State the overall inference

The family gives priority to groceries, education, clothing and transportation; rent takes a minor share.

High-Value Comparisons

Precision: The chart shows the distribution of expenditure, not household income.

Useful figures

  • Groceries exceed rent by 18 points.
  • Education and clothes differ by 1 point.
  • Clothes and transport differ by 1 point.
  • Groceries + education = 43%.
  • Top four categories = 80%.
  • Rent is under one-fourth of groceries.
