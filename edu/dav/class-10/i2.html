Analytical Paragraph from a Pie Chart

Read the data, identify the major comparisons, and organise the information into a clear 100–120 word analytical paragraph.

Question

The given pie chart represents the amount of money spent by a family on different items in a month. Write an analytical paragraph in 100–120 words using the information given in the pie chart.

Monthly Household Expenditure

Percentage distribution across six categories

All values represent the percentage share of the family’s monthly expenditure.

Chart Data

Read from highest to lowest before forming comparisons.

  • Groceries 23%
  • Education 20%
  • Clothes 19%
  • Transportation 18%
  • Miscellaneous 15%
  • Rent 5%
Before revealing the answer Identify the largest share, the smallest share, the closest values, and one meaningful combined total.

Precision note: The chart shows the distribution of expenditure, not the distribution of household income.

Answer in Discussion Points

1

Introduce the chart

State that it presents the percentage distribution of a family’s monthly expenditure across six categories.

2

Identify the highest share

Groceries account for the largest proportion at 23% of total monthly spending.

3

Explain the next major category

Education ranks second at 20%, only 3 percentage points below groceries.

4

Compare the middle categories

Clothes and transportation receive 19% and 18% respectively, making them nearly equal allocations.

5

Cover the lower shares

Miscellaneous expenses form 15%, while rent is the smallest component at just 5%.

6

Give the overall inference

The spending pattern prioritises groceries, education, clothing, and transportation, while rent takes a comparatively minor share.

Useful comparisons for the paragraph

  • Groceries exceed rent by 18 percentage points.
  • Education and clothes differ by only 1 percentage point.
  • Clothes and transportation also differ by 1 percentage point.
  • The four largest categories together account for 80% of spending.
  • Groceries and education together form 43% of total expenditure.
  • Rent is less than one-fourth of the grocery allocation.
